Les Échos — 3 November 1995

“McDonald's still a hit on the stock market” · stock market / strategy article.

Summary

A flattering stock market profile of McDonald's, “the defensive stock par excellence”: shares +41% in the United States and +28.7% in Paris since January. Nine-month results up (revenue $7.2 billion, i.e. 35.1 billion F, +19%; net profit $1 billion, +16%), driven by international operations. The article details the global strategy set out by Philippe Labbé (chief financial officer of McDonald's France).

Key figures

  • Shares: +41% (USA), +28.7% (Paris) since 1st January.
  • 9 months: revenue $7.2 billion (+19%); net profit $1 billion (+16%).
  • France: 400-restaurant mark passed; ~60 openings planned over 4-5 years.

Significance for the case file

It documents, on the basis of a reference source, the expansion strategy claimed by McDonald's France — the same logic of dense coverage which, in Antibes, would translate into the opening of competing restaurants in Collorafi's catchment area. The mention of social tensions sheds light on the balance of power at the time.
