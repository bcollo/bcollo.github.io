Summary sheet

Registered letter Collorafi to McDonald's — 27 June 1997

Overview: registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt sent by Bernard Collorafi to Denis Hennequin, chairman and chief executive of McDonald's France, against a background of litigation between the parties.

Key points

Mr Collorafi reports that he received that very morning a telephone call from Isabelle Kuster asking him not to attend a meeting (“Coop”) in Marrakech, because of the various disputes between the parties. He states that he condemns any attempt at intimidation or demobilisation, as well as the epithets he considers disparaging towards him. He announces his intention to record the incitements to exclusion of which he says he is the target and to refer them to his lawyer, expressing the hope that “dignity and intelligence” will prevail. A copy is sent to Maître Clément, counsel in Paris.

Significance

A piece of correspondence illustrating the deterioration of relations between the franchisee and the franchisor on the eve of the escalation of the dispute, and Mr Collorafi's compilation of a file for his counsel.

Bernard COLLORAFI 21 B Chemin de l'Estelle 06110 LE CANNET Le Cannet, 27 June 1997 Registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt Denis HENNEQUIN Chairman and Chief Executive Officer McDonald's France SA 1 Rue Gustave Eiffel 78045 GUYANCOURT CEDEX Dear Denis, I have just had, today at 10 a.m., a telephone call from Isabelle KUSTER who asks me not to attend the Coop de Marrakech in view of the various disputes between us. 10 909 ₫ are useful to perfect my work. Please accept, Dear Denis, my best regards. Copy: Maître CLEMENT, Lawyer PARIS
