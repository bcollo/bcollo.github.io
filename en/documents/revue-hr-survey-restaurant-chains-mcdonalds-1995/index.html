Survey of restaurant chains — Revue technique des hôtels et restaurants · January 1995

Overview: trade press article published in the Revue technique des hôtels et restaurants of January 1995, presenting the summary of a survey by Gira Sic Conseil on commercial restaurant chains in France.

Key points

Based on 1993 data, the survey identifies 84 chain brands and a total of 2,416 units, with turnover of 22.9 billion francs including VAT, i.e. 17.5% of the total turnover of French commercial catering, with nearly 40% of the brands located in Île-de-France. McDonald's comes first with 300 units and turnover of 5 billion francs, ahead of Casino-Marest (2.5 billion) and Quick (2 billion). The article reports McDonald's International's “magic formula” for assessing its expansion potential, estimated by Fortune magazine at 42,000 restaurants worldwide and 2,237 for the French market. Finally, it compares annual fast-food spending per inhabitant: 1,700 F in the United States, 160 F in France and 16 F in Italy.

Significance

Without any direct link to the dispute, this document records the market context: McDonald's dominant position and rapid growth in France at the time of the events, the framework within which the expansion decisions challenged by the franchisee were made.
