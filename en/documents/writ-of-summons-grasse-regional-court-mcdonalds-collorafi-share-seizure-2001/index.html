Writ of summons — Grasse Regional Court (TGI) · 20 June 2001

Overview: writ of summons served by a bailiff at the request of McDonald's France before the Grasse Regional Court (TGI), directed against Bernard and Olga Collorafi and their sons Sébastien and Olivier Collorafi.

Key points

The writ relies on a judgment of the Paris Court of Appeal of 8 March 2000, presented as making McDonald's France a creditor. The document recalls the joint and several orders against Mr Collorafi together with the companies SEBOL, B et O and Les Pins in respect of unpaid fees, as well as the sums payable by McDonald's in respect of tangible fixed assets and damages. McDonald's asks in particular:

  • to validate the protective attachment of company shares nos. 6001 to 12000 carried out on 22 May 2001 pursuant to an order of 10 May 2001 of the enforcement judge;
  • to convert it into an attachment for sale and to order provisional enforcement;
  • a sum of 20,000 F under article 700 of the NCPC, plus costs.

Also referred to are the SCI COLLO, registrations of mortgages and a promise of sale of 30 March 2001.

Significance

This document illustrates the forced enforcement phase of the 2000 judgment, with McDonald's seeking to recover its claims from the assets of Mr and Mrs Collorafi and their sons.

The claim of the company McDONALD's: According to the articles of association, the 6,000 shares registered in the names of Messieurs Sebastien and Olivier COLLORAFI represent a capital contribution of 600,000 FRF, that is 300,000 FRF each. However, since these were young men aged at the time of the constitution of the SCI COLLO 18 and 20 years and living with their parents, there is reason to think that they did not personally make any contribution, and that all of the contributions were made by their parents, Monsieur and Madame COLLORAFI, both at the constitution of the company and during its existence. Messieurs Sébastien and Olivier COLLORAFI are fictitious partners of the SCI COLLO constituted by Monsieur and Madame Bernard COLLORAFI and are merely nominees of their parents. In accordance with article 1843-1 of the Civil Code, "the rights of each partner in the share capital are proportional to his contributions at the constitution of the company or during its existence". In the absence of Messieurs Sébastien and Olivier COLLORAFI establishing their contributions to the SCI, it will be for the Court to rule that they are not really partners of the SCI and to say that the SCI COLLO in reality has only two partners, Monsieur Bernard COLLORAFI and Madame née BIELANSKY. Provisional enforcement will be ordered.
