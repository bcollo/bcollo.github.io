McDonald's, franchising,
the lawsuits

The Collorafi case file is not an isolated case: it is part of a history — that of the franchise agreement, of the disputes that have shaped it, and of the cases that have marked McDonald's around the world. These articles report the facts and cite their sources. They do not take sides.
